## Acceptance criteria

- Exact artifacts and staging deployments are hash-recorded and all beta-critical scenarios pass.
- No crash, ANR, data loss, duplicate payment/badge effect, inaccessible critical control, or uncleaned synthetic data remains.

## Evidence boundaries

Missing account access, signing material, device capability, or 24-hour observation is a blocker, never a waived pass.

## Deployment and rollback

Deploy staging from immutable artifacts; promote only after independent review. Roll back images/artifacts promptly and retain forward-compatible migrations.
