# Phase 34: Staging and Signed-Device Certification

## Scope

Certify the exact remediation SHA against staging services, signed Android/iOS artifacts, external integrations, and representative devices.

## Finding-to-test traceability

| Evidence area | Test | Required retained evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deployment/database | Empty and production-like migrations, recovery, rollback | Immutable image/artifact hashes and sanitized migration logs. |
| Services | PostgreSQL/Redis/R2/Firebase/APNs/email/auth/purchases | Non-secret sandbox logs and health observations. |
| Devices | Android/iOS lifecycle, accessibility, offline, performance | Signed-device matrix with OS/device/version outcomes. |
| Reliability | Queues, WebSockets, concurrency, 24-hour soak | Metrics, logs, defect disposition, and observation window. |

## Acceptance criteria

- Exact artifacts and staging deployments are hash-recorded and all beta-critical scenarios pass.
- No crash, ANR, data loss, duplicate payment/badge effect, inaccessible critical control, or uncleaned synthetic data remains.

## Evidence boundaries

Missing account access, signing material, device capability, or 24-hour observation is a blocker, never a waived pass.

## Deployment and rollback

Deploy staging from immutable artifacts; promote only after independent review. Roll back images/artifacts promptly and retain forward-compatible migrations.
