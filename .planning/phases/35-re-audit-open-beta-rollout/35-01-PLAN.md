## Acceptance criteria

- Independent reviewer issues APPROVE only after all required evidence is present and fresh.
- No blocked beta-critical scope, failed certification, or unverified artifact may advance to external beta.

## Evidence boundaries

The decision must explicitly list every unavailable external input. Static inspection and mocks cannot substitute for release certification.

## Deployment and rollback

Promote immutable artifacts in order: internal Play/TestFlight, observation, then external beta. On regression, halt promotion and roll back application artifacts while preserving safe forward migrations.
