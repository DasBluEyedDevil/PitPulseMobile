# Phase 35: Re-audit and Open-Beta Rollout

## Scope

Regenerate the audit at the remediation SHA, independently decide beta readiness, and promote exact artifacts through internal tracks before external beta.

## Finding-to-test traceability

| Evidence area | Test | Required decision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audit completeness | Regenerate ledger, hashes, matrices, scenarios, findings | 100% disposition and exact remediation SHA. |
| Release blockers | Independently reproduce every P0/P1/release P2 outcome | Zero unresolved or unverified release blockers. |
| Internal promotion | Play/TestFlight internal artifacts and 24-hour observation | Health, Sentry, queues, realtime, upload, login, and purchase data. |
| Public surface | Allowlisted health/association/legal/support fixture probes | Retained status/content-type results only. |

## Acceptance criteria

- Independent reviewer issues APPROVE only after all required evidence is present and fresh.
- No blocked beta-critical scope, failed certification, or unverified artifact may advance to external beta.

## Evidence boundaries

The decision must explicitly list every unavailable external input. Static inspection and mocks cannot substitute for release certification.

## Deployment and rollback

Promote immutable artifacts in order: internal Play/TestFlight, observation, then external beta. On regression, halt promotion and roll back application artifacts while preserving safe forward migrations.
