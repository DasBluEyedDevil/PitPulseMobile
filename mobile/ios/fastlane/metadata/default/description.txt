SoundCheck is a live music companion for fans who want to discover venues and bands, follow what is happening nearby, and remember the shows they have been part of.

Use SoundCheck to:
- Find venues and bands worth checking out
- Discover local live music activity
- Check in at concerts and save memories
- Build a profile around shows, badges, and concert credibility
- Share concert moments with friends

SoundCheck is built for invite-only beta testing while we refine discovery, profiles, check-ins, and live event features.
